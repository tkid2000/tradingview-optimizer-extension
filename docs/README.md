**PiOptimator** is the ultimate tool for automating and optimizing your TradingView strategies.
> **Note:** PiOptimator is a **fork of [OptiPie](https://optipie.app)**, designed to provide additional features for free that were previously limited or paid.

Built for traders who need efficiency and precision, PiOptimator handles the heavy lifting of backtesting, allowing you to discover the best parameters for your algorithms effortlessly with enhanced capabilities.

## 🚀 Unlock Your Strategy's Potential

Manual optimization is a thing of the past. With PiOptimator, you can:

*   **Automate Everything:** Define your parameter ranges and let the tool do the work.
*   **Data-Driven Decisions:** Access detailed reports with sorting and filtering options.
*   **Seamless Workflow:** Integrated directly into your browsing experience.
*   **Secure & Private:** Your reports and states are stored locally on your machine.

## 🛠 Features

### Smart Parameter Testing
Easily configure start, end, and step values for your strategy inputs. PiOptimator iterates through them automatically.

### Comprehensive Reporting
View, search, and analyze your optimization results in a dedicated dashboard. Export data to refine your strategies further.

### Session Persistence
Your configuration is saved automatically, so you never lose your setup even if you close the browser.

## 🌟 PiOptimator for professional-grade capabilities:

*   **Unlimited Power:** Optimize up to 20 parameters at once.
*   **Deep Analysis:** Multi-timeframe testing support.
*   **Advanced Inputs:** Support for dropdown and checkbox parameters.
*   **Flexible Control:** Pause and resume optimizations or stop early with partial results.
